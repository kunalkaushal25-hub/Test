How a deal moves

Six stages, one owner, one next step

  1. Lead capturedPortal, WhatsApp, walk-in or broker referral. Duplicates merged.
  2. Site visitVisit booked and logged, with the units the buyer viewed.
  3. EOI receivedExpression of interest paid and the unit held for 72 hours.
  4. BookingBooking amount collected and payment plan agreed.
  5. SPA signedSales and purchase agreement signed by both parties.
  6. Oqood registeredOff-plan sale registered with the land department. Deal closed.
